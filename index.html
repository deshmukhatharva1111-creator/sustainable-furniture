Giving Cardboard a Second Life

GREENCRAFT is a sustainable furniture project focused on converting reusable hard cardboard into a practical foldable storage rack.

Large amounts of cardboard packaging are discarded every day. Instead of allowing suitable cardboard to become waste, this project explores how it can be redesigned into useful furniture.

The rack is designed with portability, space efficiency, affordability and material reuse in mind.

Our idea promotes responsible consumption and demonstrates how creative engineering can support a more sustainable lifestyle.

Foldable Cardboard Rack

The proposed furniture is a storage rack manufactured from reused hard cardboard sheets. Its structure is designed to provide organized storage for lightweight household or study materials.

The foldable concept makes it easier to store or move the rack when it is not required.

Careful cutting, folding and reinforcement help improve the shape and stability of the structure.

Manufacturing and Assembly Process

From discarded cardboard to useful furniture

1

Collection

Collect clean, dry and reusable hard cardboard sheets.

2

Measurement

Measure the required dimensions and prepare a layout for the rack components.

3

Cutting

Cut the cardboard panels carefully according to the prepared design.

4

Reinforcement

Reinforce important areas with additional cardboard layers where needed.

5

Joining

Join the panels and assemble the rack structure using the selected joining method.

6

Inspection

Check folding operation, alignment and stability. Test with light loads before regular use.

Smart Use of Space

The foldable concept allows the furniture to be collapsed or rearranged when it is not in use, depending on the final joint and panel design.

Limitations and Safety

Understanding the material is important

  • Cardboard can lose strength when exposed to moisture.
  • Load capacity depends on material quality, thickness, joints and construction.
  • The rack should initially be used only for lightweight items.
  • It should not be used for heavy or fragile objects without proper structural testing.
  • Keep the furniture away from water and damp areas.
