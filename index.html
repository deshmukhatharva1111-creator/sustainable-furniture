ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is Sustainable Furniture?

Sustainable furniture focuses on designing and producing furniture while reducing environmental impact and making responsible use of resources.

It encourages the use of renewable, recycled, reclaimed and durable materials, along with thoughtful manufacturing and long-lasting design.

Our project explores how furniture can combine functionality, appearance and environmental responsibility.

LOOKING AHEAD

Future Scope

Future development can include smart modular furniture, improved recycling techniques, biodegradable materials, repairable designs, digital manufacturing and life-cycle assessment.

The goal is to develop furniture that is functional, affordable, durable and environmentally responsible.
