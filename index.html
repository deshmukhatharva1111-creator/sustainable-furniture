THE IDEA BEHIND GREENCRAFT

A New Life for Old Cardboard

GREENCRAFT is a sustainable furniture innovation project that explores the conversion of discarded hard cardboard into practical furniture.

Our main product concept is a foldable storage rack. It is intended to provide a convenient way to organize lightweight objects while encouraging the reuse of materials that might otherwise be thrown away.

Responsible Material Selection

Giving suitable discarded cardboard another purpose.

Practical Product Design

Combining storage utility with a foldable structure.

Creative Engineering

Exploring simple construction and innovative connections.

WHY THIS PROJECT MATTERS

The Problem We Want to Address

Cardboard packaging is commonly discarded after its original purpose has been completed. At the same time, many storage products require new materials and manufacturing resources.

📦

Cardboard Waste

Used cardboard boxes and packaging can become unnecessary waste when they are not reused or recycled appropriately.

🌍

Resource Consumption

Manufacturing new products requires materials, energy and other resources. Reusing existing materials can help reduce the demand for new inputs.

FROM WASTE TO PRODUCT

Manufacturing and Assembly

The proposed manufacturing process follows a sequence of preparation, cutting, joining, folding and inspection.

01

Material Collection

Collect reusable cardboard boxes and sheets. Select dry, clean and undamaged material.

02

Design and Measurement

Prepare the rack layout and decide the dimensions of its shelves and side panels.

03

Cutting and Preparation

Mark and cut cardboard components according to the planned dimensions.

04

Panel Reinforcement

Strengthen important panels and joints according to the structural design.

05

Assembly and Joining

Connect the prepared components to create the main storage rack structure.

06

Folding Arrangement

Incorporate the planned folding connections and check the opening and closing movement.

07

Surface Finishing

Apply appropriate surface finishing for a neat and presentable appearance.
